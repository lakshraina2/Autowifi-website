How The Extension Works

01

Intelligent Detection

AutoWifi runs silently in the background. It monitors network requests to detect when you are redirected to a captive portal (like Fortinet or Sophos gateways). It instantly recognizes the login page structure.

02

Secure Injection

Once detected, the extension retrieves your encrypted credentials from your device's local storage. We use the chrome.storage.local API, ensuring your password never leaves your browser.

03

Keep-Alive Protocol

To prevent your university network from disconnecting you due to "inactivity," AutoWifi sends periodic heartbeat packets. This ensures your large downloads or gaming sessions are never interrupted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my password safe?

Absolutely. AutoWifi operates on a "Zero-Knowledge" architecture. Your username and password are stored locally on your specific device. We do not have servers, and we never transmit your credentials over the internet.

Does it work on all Wi-Fi?

AutoWifi is optimized for captive portals that use standard HTML forms (like 172.16.x.x gateways). It works best on University, Hotel, and Airport networks.

Why not use Chrome Autofill?

Chrome Autofill only fills the text boxes; it doesn't click "Login" for you. AutoWifi detects the page, fills the data, and submits the form instantly without you touching the keyboard.

Is it free?

Yes! The core features of AutoWifi are free for all students. We are supported by unobtrusive ads on our download pages. Our mission is to make campus internet accessible.
