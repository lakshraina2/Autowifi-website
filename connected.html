Weekly Feature: Safety First

You are now connected to a public network. While AutoWifi handles the login, here is what you need to know about keeping your data safe on campus Wi-Fi.

Does Incognito Mode Protect Me?

Many students think Incognito Mode hides their activity from the college admin. It does not. Incognito only stops your browser from saving history. The network admin can still see every site you visit unless you use HTTPS or a VPN.

Why "HTTPS" Matters

Always look for the "lock" icon 🔒 in your address bar. This means your data is encrypted between you and the website. If you see "Not Secure," do not enter passwords or credit card info.
